The origin of the Uruguayan Murga

the uruguayan murga is a one of the mos important and dears celebrations in the culture of Uruguay. is a type of music and popular theather popular that is presented during the Carnival festival, especially in the Montevideo city. For the summer months the Uruguay, group of singers climb of scenography of the neighborhood for generait smile, laugh, think and excite all families spectacter. since 1906. in this age, coming of uruguay one group of theather from de city of Cadiz ,España. this group of spanish artist traveled for the word presented her musical plays. for bad lucky, the company didn't do very well in the city of Montevideo. a very small lot of people buyed the ticket for his show. whit didnt generated money, the artist dont havent money for buy ticket of trip and come back his home in spain. For can eat and save money for his comeback trip, the spanish artist taked a descicion: go to the street of the city. They started singing and act in the places and avenue. They went by the name of “Murga la Gaditana” ( the name 'Gaditana' represents a person who is from Cadiz). the neighboords see and gave them coins to help them. The Pepleo of the city disfruted the form what spanish singin and make jokes in the street. But this in the coming year, a group of Uruguayans imited the spains artist. the young artist painted his faces, They put on old costumes and went out to sing. As a joke, they called their group “Murga La Gaditana que se va” This is how the first murga in Uruguay was born. That was the oficial birth of the murga in Uruguay. From this moment, the idea growed very fast. The uruguayans taked the spanish idea, but they added his own rhythms of drums and her local customs. How is the Murga Today? With the pass of the years, the murga formed his own style. Today, a group have almost twenty persons. They use only three musical instruments basics for make the rhythm: The bombo (a big drum) The platillo (two metal discs what crash) The redoblante (a smaller drum) The rest of the sound is pure singing of a chorus with very strong voices. The show of a murga have parts very easy for understand. First, they make a initial greeting for say hello to the public. Then, come the principal part, where they sing about the news of the year, make jokes about the politicians and tell stories of the daily life. For last, arrive the farewell. This is the most sweet part, where the murga say goodbye until the next year. Today, the costumes of the murga are very brilliant and colorful. The artists pass many months making her clothes and thinking in how paint his faces. The murga now is dont only of Montevideo; is a symbol of all Uruguay and is very easy for love fuente: historiadora uruguaya Milita Alfaro, especialmente de su libro "Carnaval: Una historia de Montevideo"
